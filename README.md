# Elias Asmus

Electrical engineering student at TUM in Munich. I design low-noise analog electronics for MOVE Prosthetics, our student club at TUM.

### Projects

- **Cacao AFE**: two-channel surface-EMG front end around the TI ADS1299-4 for MOVE Prosthetics' myoelectric hand. A 4-layer Altium design with a floating battery supply, separate analog and digital LDOs, and a driven-bias (DRL) network for 50 Hz rejection.
- **WARR flight electronics**: schematics and layouts in Altium for WARR's sounding rockets. Tested and debugged the EX-5 flight electronics for the European Rocketry Challenge (EuRoC) 2026.
